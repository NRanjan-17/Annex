## Annex 0.1.8

### Fixed

- **Extend display onto a 4.5K or 5K screen**, such as an iMac, showed no picture: the picture was bigger than the video format allows, so every frame failed. Annex now sends it at 4096 × 2304, the largest the format carries, and the other computer scales it to fit.
- **If the video can't be encoded, the session ends with a message** instead of leaving a black screen.
- **Mac: Extend steps in the log.** With *Save a log file* on, annex.log now shows each step of an Extend session on the sharing Mac and any error, to help find why a picture doesn't arrive.
