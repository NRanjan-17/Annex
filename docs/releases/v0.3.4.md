## Annex 0.3.4

**Install it on both computers.** Each side takes its own echo out, so both need 0.3.4.

### Fixed

- **No more echo with the microphone and speakers on.** With *Send my microphone* on, each side used to hear itself come back. Annex now cancels the echo the way video-call apps do: the computer you view from takes the other computer's sound out of your microphone, and the computer you view takes your voice out of the sound it sends back. Headphones are no longer needed.
- **Your microphone sounds cleaner.** Steady background noise, like a fan, is filtered out of it.
- **Music and videos are left alone.** The computer's own sound is only touched for a moment while your voice plays on it, and never filtered for noise.
