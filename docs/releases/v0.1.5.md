## Annex 0.1.5

### New

- **About and Paired devices pages.** Both have their own place at the bottom of the sidebar. Settings keeps General, Sharing, Viewing and Developer.
- **Updates on the About page.** The app, its version and links at the top, with all update controls underneath: automatic checks, beta updates, the current status, Check now, and when Annex last checked.
- **A new app icon:** two screens in white on black.
- **Windows: a two-tone sidebar** that folds to icons with the menu button, like Windows Settings. Share this PC shows a dot while you're sharing.
