## Annex 0.2.0-pre.2

A pre-release for testing: turn on **Beta updates** (About → Updates) to get it. Thanks to [Saidev Dhal](https://saidev.codes). Everything in [0.2.0-pre.1](https://github.com/NRanjan-17/Annex/releases/tag/v0.2.0-pre.1) is included.

### New since pre.1

- **A redesigned Windows app to match the Mac.** Black and white, over the desktop's acrylic, with its own sidebar: **Nearby**, **This PC** and **Paired devices**, with Settings and About at the bottom.
  - **Nearby** replaces Connect and the per-computer pages: pick a computer for Extend, Show its screen, Direct Wi-Fi, mouse and keyboard, or Forget, or double-click it to connect. While nothing is found, it shows the firewall and network help. A bar shows the live session's delay and frame rate.
  - **This PC** has one Share switch. **Connect by address** is a dialog.
  - **The tray menu** works like the Mac's menu bar popover: a sharing switch, the live session, and nearby computers with Extend and View.
- **Mac:** the app icon in the sidebar, the menu bar popover and the Nearby waiting panel.
- **Docs:** a proposal for connecting over the internet without a VPN ([docs/INTERNET-CONNECT.md](https://github.com/NRanjan-17/Annex/blob/main/docs/INTERNET-CONNECT.md)), for discussion; nothing in the app changes.
