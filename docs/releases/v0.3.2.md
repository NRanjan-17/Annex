## Annex 0.3.2

**Install it on both computers.** Sound and the smaller picture need 0.3.2 on each side.

### New

- **Sound.** You hear the computer you're viewing, and you can send your microphone back. On Linux your microphone appears there as **Annex Microphone**, so you can pick it in a call. Find the switches in Settings → Advanced → Sound. The Mac asks for microphone permission the first time you turn on "Send my microphone".
- **Pause or unsend a file.** Files you're sending show Pause and Unsend in the island at the top of the screen; files arriving show Stop. Unsending deletes what had already arrived.
- **Annex for Linux looks and works like the Mac.** A dark window with a sidebar, grouped settings, the Quality picker, update checks, live session stats, the code's countdown with a Copy button, and this computer's addresses.

### Changed

- **Smooth video on slow connections.** On a slow link Annex lowers the bitrate further and sends a smaller picture, so the video stays live instead of falling seconds behind. The full picture comes back when the link speeds up.
- **Verbose logging is on by default** on every platform, so a first report already says what went wrong. Turn it off in Settings → Developer.
- **Linux keeps both computers awake during a session**, and Esc leaves a view-only session, as on the Mac.
- **Annex waits longer for its fast connection** before falling back to one that can't send files, which helps slower computers.

### Fixed

- **Mac: the notch glows as soon as you drag a file**, and the island hangs right from the camera with no gap.
- **Mac: the menu bar icon is solid while connected** and faded otherwise.
- **Mac: a failed connection now says why**, on Nearby and in the menu bar.
