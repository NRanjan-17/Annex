## Annex 0.2.0-pre.1

A pre-release for testing: turn on **Beta updates** (About → Updates) to get it. Thanks to [Saidev Dhal](https://saidev.codes) for most of this release.

### New

- **A redesigned Mac app.** Black, with Liquid Glass on what floats (the sidebar, the selected computer's card, the live-session bar). Three places instead of six: **Nearby**, **This Mac** and **Paired Devices**. Connect by address and a PC's Direct Wi-Fi are in a sheet. On macOS before 26 it uses the standard translucent material.
- **Nearby on the Mac** shows how each computer is reached (Wi-Fi, Cable, Direct Wi-Fi, Peer-to-peer) and connects with a double-click or Return. While nothing is found, it says what to do on the other computer.
- **A new menu bar popover:** one switch to share this Mac, the live session with its delay and frame rate, and every nearby computer with Extend and View.
- **Mac ↔ Mac with no router.** Macs on different networks, or none, find and connect to each other over peer-to-peer Wi-Fi, the link AirDrop uses. Only paired Macs see a sharing Mac; **This Mac → Macs with no network → Allow** shows it to every Mac nearby for 5 minutes, to pair a new one. It goes quiet during a session and can be turned off in Settings → Sharing.
- **Mac: join a PC's Direct Wi-Fi by hand**, for networks that keep computers apart: enter the network name and password the PC shows under **Share → Direct Wi-Fi**.
- **Over the internet:** a guide to connecting through Tailscale or ZeroTier ([docs/REMOTE.md](https://github.com/NRanjan-17/Annex/blob/main/docs/REMOTE.md)). Not yet tested end to end.

### Fixed

- **Mac Extend: no more black screen** when the encoder gave no frames: the stream now stays within what the hardware encoder delivers (a 4.5K iMac gets 3840 × 2160 at 60 fps), Annex falls back to another encoder if one stays silent, and capture restarts once macOS has set up the new display.
- **Connecting over a cable or IPv6** works again: the sharing computer now listens on IPv6 as well as IPv4.
- **The Mac window can go full screen again.**
- The log (Settings → Developer → Save a log file) now records more of each session on both sides.
