## Annex 0.2.0-pre.6

A pre-release for testing: turn on **Beta updates** (About → Updates) to get it. Everything in pre.1 to pre.5 is included.

### New since pre.5

- **Mac: Extended display scaling in Settings → Sharing.** Choose how big text and windows are on a screen extending this Mac: **Sharpest** (100 %, or 200 % on a high-resolution screen) or a step from 100 % to 200 %. It applies at once, and every time that computer extends this Mac.

### Changed since pre.5

- **Mac: a screen extending this Mac starts at its sharpest scaling** instead of copying the other computer's own (such as a PC's 125 %), which looked a little soft.
