## Annex 0.1.1 pre-release 1

A test build. Please report what you find.

### New

- **Direct Wi-Fi, two ways.** Turn on **Connect over direct Wi-Fi** (Windows: Connect page; Mac: on a computer's page) to move each session onto the PC's hotspot right after it connects. Or switch a running session from either computer: the viewer's **Switch to direct Wi-Fi**, or the sharing computer's **Switch to direct Wi-Fi** next to each viewer. The Windows PC always runs the hotspot.
- **Direct Wi-Fi switch on Windows.** Share this PC has a Direct Wi-Fi switch that runs the hotspot and shows its name and password.
- **Refresh Nearby.** A refresh button next to Nearby re-checks the list: computers that went away drop off.
- **Disk image.** The Mac disk image opens a clean window with just Annex and Applications. The uninstaller is now in Annex: **About → Uninstall…**.

### Fixed

- A computer that stopped and started sharing no longer disappears from Nearby on the other computer.
- After switching to direct Wi-Fi, the viewer reconnects as soon as the other computer joins the hotspot, instead of waiting for the old connection to time out.
- Events from a closed viewer can no longer end the new session that replaced it.
- The Mac no longer stays extended onto a black display when Screen Recording is off or capture fails.
- If Smart App Control blocks Annex on Windows, it says so instead of showing a generic crash.
- The log now records panics, why a session ended, and why QUIC fell back to TCP.
- Updated a dependency for a security advisory (RUSTSEC-2026-0385).
