## Annex 0.3.1

**Install it on both computers.** After updating, Annex shows a short "What's new" once; it's also on the About page.

### New

- **Copy here, paste there.** While you control another computer, text and pictures you copy on one can be pasted on the other. Passwords copied from password managers stay on the computer they were copied on. Turn it off in Settings → Clipboard and files.
- **Send files by dragging them** onto the window showing the other computer. They arrive in its Downloads folder, checked so they're exactly what was sent.
- **Drop islands.** Start dragging a file and a black island with a silver edge pops out at the top of the screen: from the notch on a Mac, from the top on Windows and on Linux (KDE, Sway, Labwc and X11 desktops). Bring the file close and drop it to send it. On GNOME (Wayland) there's no island; drop files on the viewer window.
- **Scrolling feels the same everywhere.** A Mac with natural scrolling now scrolls the same way when it controls Windows or Linux. Change it in Settings → Viewing.
- **What's new after each update**, and a What's New button on the About page.

### Changed

- **A log that explains what went wrong.** Every feature now logs what it does. When something fails, the moments before it are saved in the `crashes` folder. Turn on **Verbose logging** while chasing a problem, and use **Show log files** to find them. Only your account can read the log, and it never holds what you type, copy or send, or your PINs and codes.
- **Mac: small scrolls on a Mac you control no longer judder.**
- **Linux Extend uses the viewer's scale**, so text on the extra display is the right size.
- **Connect with a code** has a close button and starts empty every time you open it.

### Fixed

- **Mac: looking for nearby computers no longer opens Tailscale.**
