## Annex 0.3.9

**Install it on both computers.**

### New

- **Mac shortcuts go to the Mac you control.** While the viewer is in front and you're controlling, ⌘Space (Spotlight), ⌘Tab, ⌃← and ⌃→ (desktops), Mission Control, and the screenshot keys work on the other Mac instead of yours. Click anywhere outside the viewer, or swipe to another desktop with four fingers, and they're yours again. ⌃⌥⇧Q still disconnects.
  - If Annex quits unexpectedly, your shortcuts come back on their own straight away.
  - Four-finger swipes stay on your Mac: macOS doesn't let any app pass them on. ⌃←, ⌃→ and ⌃↑ do the same on the other Mac.

### Improved

- **Scrolling turns the way it does on your Mac.** The Reverse Mouse Wheel setting is gone. On a Mac, Linux or Windows computer you control, scrolling follows your Mac's own natural-scrolling setting, with a trackpad and a mouse wheel alike.
- **Windows: the app is built on the newest Windows App SDK (2.6).**
