## Annex 0.3.9

Everything since 0.3.8, including the 0.3.9 betas. **Install it on both computers.**

### New

- **Your shortcuts go to the computer you control.** While the viewer is in front and you're controlling, they work on the other computer instead of yours. Click anywhere outside the viewer and they're yours again.
  - **Mac:** ⌘Space (Spotlight), ⌘Tab, ⌃← and ⌃→ (desktops), Mission Control, and the screenshot keys. Swiping to another desktop with four fingers also gives them back. Four-finger swipes stay on your Mac: macOS doesn't let any app pass them on, so use ⌃←, ⌃→ and ⌃↑ instead. ⌃⌥⇧Q disconnects.
  - **Windows:** the Windows key, Alt+Tab, Alt+Esc, Ctrl+Esc and Print Screen. Ctrl+Alt+Del and Windows+L always stay with your PC, as Windows requires. Ctrl+Alt+Shift+Q disconnects.
  - **Linux:** Super, Alt+Tab and your desktop's other shortcuts. They follow control as the other computer allows it or takes it back. Your desktop may ask once whether Annex can take them. Ctrl+Alt+Shift+Q disconnects.
  - If Annex quits unexpectedly, your shortcuts are yours again straight away.

### Improved

- **Scrolling turns the way it does on your Mac.** The Reverse Mouse Wheel setting is gone. On a Mac, Linux or Windows computer you control, scrolling follows your Mac's own natural-scrolling setting, with a trackpad and a mouse wheel alike.
- **Windows: built on the newest Windows App SDK (2.6).**
