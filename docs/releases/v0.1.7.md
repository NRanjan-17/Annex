## Annex 0.1.7

### New

- **Scaling for Extend display on the Mac.** System Settings → Displays offers *Larger Text* to *More Space* for the Annex Display, the same steps as Windows' 100 % to 200 %. It starts at the other computer's own scaling.
- **Refresh rates for Extend display.** The Annex Display offers the same refresh rates as the screen it appears on, and starts at that screen's current rate: a 144 Hz laptop gets 144 Hz. Choose another rate in System Settings → Displays on a Mac, or Settings → System → Display on Windows.

### Fixed

- **Screens no longer go to sleep during a session.** While you're extending or mirroring, neither computer turns its screen off or sleeps for lack of input.
