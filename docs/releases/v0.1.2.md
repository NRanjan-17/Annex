## Annex 0.1.2

### Fixed

- **Windows updates now stay where Annex is installed.** In 0.1.1, updating an all-users install (for example on another drive) could put a second copy in your user folder, without the Extend driver. Updating to 0.1.2 puts the update on your existing install, asks Windows for permission once, keeps the Extend driver, and removes the extra copy. Your settings and paired devices stay.
- **Nearby heading on Windows.** The sidebar shows the Nearby heading again, with a Refresh entry at the end of the list.
- "Start with Windows" follows Annex if it moves.

If you have two Annex entries in Windows Settings → Apps, don't uninstall either one by hand: uninstalling also deletes your settings and paired devices. Update to 0.1.2 instead and the extra copy goes away.
