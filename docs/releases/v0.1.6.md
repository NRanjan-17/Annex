## Annex 0.1.6

### Changed

- **One look on every page.** Settings, Paired devices and the other pages now use the About page's grouped rows on both Mac and Windows.
- **Paired devices:** each device has its own group, with Remove and whether it can use this computer's mouse and keyboard. On the Mac, the page header is back.
- **Windows installer:** the setup wizard shows the Annex artwork.
- **Clearer update dialog on the Mac:** Annex quits, updates and opens again, and its permissions stay as they are.

### Fixed

- **Windows:** with the sidebar folded, the page was pushed to the right and cut off at the edge. It now fills the window.
