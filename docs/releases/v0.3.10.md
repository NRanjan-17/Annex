## Annex 0.3.10

Scrolling fixes. **Install it on both computers.**

### Fixed

- **A Mac trackpad no longer scrolls backwards on another Mac.** Controlling a Mac from a Mac, the trackpad and the Magic Mouse now scroll the way they do on your own Mac. A mouse wheel on Windows or Linux controlling a Mac turns the way it does on that PC.
- **Reverse Mouse Wheel is back** (Settings → Advanced → Viewing, on by default). Controlling a Windows PC or a Linux computer from a Mac with natural scrolling on, a plain mouse wheel turns the way it does on a PC. The trackpad and the Magic Mouse always keep natural scrolling.
