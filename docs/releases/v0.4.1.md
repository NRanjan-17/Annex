## Annex 0.4.1

A fix for a black screen when a Windows PC shares. **Install it on the PC that shares; on both computers is best.**

### Fixed

- **No more black screen from a Windows PC.** On some PCs, often laptops with two graphics chips (for example AMD and NVIDIA), the session connected and the pointer moved, but no picture ever came: the PC's video encoder took every frame and sent nothing back, or never marked a full picture. Annex now finds full pictures in the video itself, and if an encoder produces nothing within two seconds it switches to H.264, then to the software encoder.

### Improved

- **The log says why a picture doesn't arrive.** If no video goes out in the first three seconds, `annex.log` names the likely cause, the viewer's log says the connection works but no video came, and the PC's log names the video encoder it chose.
- **Saidev Dhal is credited on Windows too:** the installer and Apps & features now name both makers, Nalinish Ranjan and Saidev Dhal.
