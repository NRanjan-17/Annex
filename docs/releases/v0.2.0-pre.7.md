## Annex 0.2.0-pre.7

A pre-release for testing: turn on **Beta updates** (About → Updates) to get it. Everything in pre.1 to pre.6 is included. **Install it on both computers.**

### New since pre.6

- **In Extend, the pointer can leave the extended screen, like a real monitor.** While you control the other computer, your mouse now moves its pointer across all its screens: off the Annex Display onto its own screen, and back. It works in every direction: Mac and Windows, either way round. While you're controlling, your own pointer stays hidden in the viewer; press **⌃⌥⇧Q** (Mac) or **Ctrl+Alt+Shift+Q** (Windows) to leave. Mirror works as before.
