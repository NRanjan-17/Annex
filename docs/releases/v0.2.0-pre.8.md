## Annex 0.2.0-pre.8

A pre-release for testing: turn on **Beta updates** (About → Updates) to get it. Thanks to [Saidev Dhal](https://saidev.codes). Everything in pre.1 to pre.7 is included. **Install it on both computers.**

### New since pre.7

- **Share and connect over the internet with a code.** You can now share or view a screen across different networks and over the internet without setting up a VPN or touching router settings.
  - On the computer you want to share, go to **This Mac** or **This PC**, find **Over the internet**, and click **Get a code**. Annex creates a short 6-character code (valid for 10 minutes) with a live countdown.
  - On the viewing computer, choose **Connect by Code or Address** (from Nearby or the menu bar / tray) and enter the code.
  - Annex opens the connection directly through home routers whenever possible using STUN. If both sides are behind strict routers that block direct traffic, Annex falls back to an encrypted relay: your own TURN server (Settings → Internet) or our built-in relay (up to 20 sessions a day, 20 minutes each).
  - Internet connections are opt-in and off by default. Connecting over your own local network never contacts the rendezvous server.

### Changed since pre.7

- **Mac: viewer title bar sits above the picture.** The title bar no longer covers the top edge of the remote screen, so its full menu bar and controls remain visible and clickable. It names the computer, shows whether the link is direct, over the internet, or relayed, and has a Disconnect button.
- **Menu bar and tray icons show state.** The Mac menu bar icon and Windows system tray icon are faded when sharing is turned off, solid when sharing is active, and show a green dot while a session is live.
- **Simpler Nearby screen.** Active sessions sit right at the top. The manual refresh button is gone because discovery happens automatically, and one **Connect by Code or Address** button opens the connection sheet.
- **Headless CLI:** `annex host --internet` and `annex viewer --code <CODE>` allow testing and scriptable internet sessions without a UI.
