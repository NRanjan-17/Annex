## Annex 0.3.9-pre.2

A pre-release for testing: turn on **Beta updates** (About → Updates) to get it. **Install it on both computers.**

### New

- **Windows and Linux shortcuts go to the computer you control, as on a Mac.** While the viewer is in front and you're controlling, they work on the other computer instead of yours. Click outside the viewer and they're yours again. Ctrl+Alt+Shift+Q still disconnects.
  - **Windows:** the Windows key, Alt+Tab, Alt+Esc, Ctrl+Esc and Print Screen. Ctrl+Alt+Del and Windows+L always stay with your PC, as Windows requires. If Annex quits unexpectedly, your shortcuts are yours again straight away.
  - **Linux:** Super, Alt+Tab and your desktop's other shortcuts. They now follow control as the other computer allows it or takes it back, not only when the window first opens. Your desktop may ask once whether Annex can take them.
